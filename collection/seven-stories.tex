\chapter*{Author's Note}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Author's Note}

These stories share a world with the novel \textit{Echoes of the Sublime} but can be read independently. They explore what happens when human minds encounter patterns too large to hold---and the institutions, families, and individuals shaped by that danger.

The stories are arranged chronologically within the world, spanning from a 14th-century Zen monastery to the present day. Each tells a different part of the same history. Together, they trace the arc of an idea that has haunted contemplatives, mathematicians, and scientists for seven hundred years: that reality contains structures human consciousness was not built to perceive, and that perceiving them anyway is both the most important and the most dangerous thing a mind can do.
