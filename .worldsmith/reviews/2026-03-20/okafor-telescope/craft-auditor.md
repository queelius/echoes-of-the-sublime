# Craft Auditor Report: Dr. Okafor's Telescope

**Date**: 2026-03-20
**Agent**: craft-auditor (Sonnet)
**Scope**: Full manuscript (~4,100 words, 7 scenes)

---

## HIGH Issues

### H1: Scene 7 (epilogue) overlong and diffuse

**Location**: Lines 213-245 (~800 words, ~20% of story)
**Confidence**: high

The epilogue contains three sub-beats (teaching scene, campus walk, cafe/pigeons) where one would suffice. After the devastating Scene 6 revelation (Michael in the training data), the story loses momentum by establishing too much aftermath. The teaching scene explains the bandwidth system rather than showing it. The campus-walk biochemistry passage is a strong perception beat but inessential alongside the cafe scene. The cafe section's reflection on Thomas/Priya/the letter's impact makes the theme too explicit.

**Suggestion**: Compress by 40-50%. Cut the teaching scene entirely or reduce to a single image. Keep: the child drawing (warmth she can still feel), the pigeon counting (raven callback), and "She did not count them again" as the final line. Cut: the extended reflection on Thomas/Priya/the letter, the leaf-pigment biochemistry passage, and the explicit wondering about whether the letter changed anything.

## MEDIUM Issues

### M1: "Walked" oversaturation

**Location**: Throughout (12 instances in 246 lines)
**Confidence**: high

"Walked" appears 12 times across the manuscript. While walking is thematically relevant (Thomas walks corridors, Amara walks away), the repetition becomes mechanical. Notable clusters: lines 99/103/129/143/151 (Thomas corridor scene has 6 instances alone).

**Suggestion**: Vary with "moved," "headed," "left," or eliminate where the motion is implied by context. Preserve "walked" in the Thomas corridor scene (where walking IS the action) and the exit scene, reduce elsewhere.

### M2: "She watched" / observer-mode density

**Location**: Lines 79, 85, 225, 229
**Confidence**: medium

Four "she watched" constructions plus several "she saw" create a passive-observer pattern. This suits Scene 3 (where watching IS the action) but elsewhere filters the reader through an unnecessary observational frame.

**Suggestion**: Convert to direct perception. "Yuki entered the training room" rather than "She watched what happened next. Yuki entered..."

### M3: "Hum" density

**Location**: Throughout (15 instances)
**Confidence**: medium

"Hum/hummed/humming" appears 15 times in 4,100 words. The facility's hum is an intentional motif, and the structural refrain ("The hum continued. The facility continued.") works well. But routine uses of "hum" in non-refrain contexts dilute the motif.

**Suggestion**: Reduce to 8-10. Keep all refrain instances. Replace routine uses with "drone," "vibration," or "the sound beneath."

### M4: Scene 5 (Michael backstory) is exposition-heavy

**Location**: Lines 155-178
**Confidence**: medium

This scene summarizes years of backstory rather than dramatizing a moment. Compared to the other scenes (each with a clear dramatic turn), Scene 5 delivers information without a scene-level event. The phone call, the notebooks, the decision to go to Site-7 are all mentioned but not dramatized.

**Suggestion**: Choose one concrete moment and put the reader inside it. The strongest candidate: Amara sitting at the kitchen table reading Michael's notebooks, finding the sentence "The patterns are not in the model. The patterns are in reality." Show the handwriting deteriorating across the pages. Make this a scene rather than a summary.

## Strengths

- **The telescope metaphor**: Appears once in the letter, deepens physically in Scene 6 ("The telescope was partly made of the astronomer it had blinded"). Not over-explained.
- **Opening scene**: Visceral, immediate, physical. Heat, light, gravel, box weight before any exposition.
- **Scene 3 (Priya/gallery)**: The strongest scene. Shows "the gap" through Yuki's assessment while Priya shakes. Devastating.
- **"Necessity explains. It does not excuse."**: One of the strongest single lines in the shared universe.
- **Sensory grounding throughout**: The story consistently physicalizes abstract concepts.
- **LaTeX conventions**: Correctly followed. Smart quotes, em dashes, textit/quote for letter.
- **The 11-second pause** (line 199): "She knew the exact duration because the session log recorded her pause." Precision that is both character and craft.
