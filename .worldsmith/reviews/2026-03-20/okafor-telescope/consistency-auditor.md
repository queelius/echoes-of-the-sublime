## HIGH Issues

### H1: "Three years" vs. canonical "five years" at Site-7

**Location**: Lines 3, 11, 29, 73, 173
**Confidence**: high

The story consistently states Amara spent "three years" at Site-7:
- "which had survived three years and two sublevels" (line 3)
- "she had spent three years learning not to hear it" (line 11)
- "Three years of living inside a machine that never slept" (line 29)
- "Fourteen times, across three years" (line 73)
- "She spent three years looking through the lenses" (line 173)

**Canonical source**: characters.md states "recruited early 2011 (after brother Michael's death), departed ~2016." The work-specific lore (worldbuilding.md) explicitly establishes "Early 2011: Amara recruited to the Order, arriving at Site-7" and departure ~2016 = 5 years.

**However**: The manuscript's internal timeline is self-consistent. Line 157-159: "Not in the first year after his death... Not in the second year... She came in the third year." If Michael died early 2011 and she came "in the third year" (~2013), then 3 years at Site-7 = departure ~2016. The manuscript's logic works, but contradicts the spinoff's own lore docs and the parent characters.md.

**Assessment**: Either the docs or the manuscript must be reconciled. If arrival is 2013, update all docs. If arrival is 2011, change "three years" to "five years" throughout and adjust "the third year" backstory passage.
