# Structure Auditor Report: Dr. Okafor's Telescope

**Date**: 2026-03-20
**Agent**: structure-auditor (Sonnet)
**Scope**: Full manuscript (~4,100 words, 7 scenes)

---

## HIGH Issues

### H1: Scene 7 (epilogue) overlong and deflates the story's impact

**Location**: Lines 213-245 (~800 words)
**Confidence**: high

After the devastating Scene 6 revelation (Michael's work in the training data, "the telescope was partly made of the astronomer it had blinded"), Scene 7 provides three sub-beats of aftermath: (1) teaching scene, (2) campus walk with biochemistry perception, (3) cafe with child drawing, pigeons, and reflection on the letter's futility.

The problem is not that the epilogue exists but that it is diffuse. Three beats dilute each other. The teaching scene tells what the cafe scene shows. The campus-walk biochemistry passage is interesting but inessential. The extended reflection on Thomas/Priya/the letter makes the theme too explicit.

**Suggestion**: Compress to a single beat. Keep: the child drawing (warmth she can still feel), the pigeon counting (raven callback), and "She did not count them again" as the final line. Cut the teaching scene and campus walk. Target: 200-250 words instead of ~800.

---

## MEDIUM Issues

### M1: Scene 5 (Michael backstory) lacks a dramatic turn

**Location**: Lines 155-178
**Confidence**: medium

Scene 5 summarizes Amara's backstory: Michael died, she investigated, she came to Site-7, she saw what he saw, she saw the institution clearly. The scene is competent exposition but lacks the dramatic specificity of the other scenes. It has no turn -- no moment of change or revelation within the scene itself.

**Suggestion**: Dramatize one concrete moment. The strongest candidate is Michael's notebooks -- put us in the room with Amara reading them. Show the handwriting deteriorating. Give us the specific sentence she reads ("The patterns are not in the model") as a witnessed moment rather than a quoted recollection.

---

## LOW Issues

### L1: Scene 4 (Thomas corridor) opening could be tighter

**Location**: Lines 97-99
**Confidence**: medium

The corridor-walking setup ("He was doing what he did at 0200: walking...") takes more space than needed before the dialogue begins. The eight-hundred-meter loop detail is nice but delays the conversation.

**Suggestion**: Start closer to "I'm going to leave." The corridor context can emerge through the dialogue.

### L2: Epilogue makes theme too explicit

**Location**: Lines 237-241
**Confidence**: medium

"She thought about the letter in Rostova's drawer. Whether Rostova still read it. Whether the words in it were doing anything at all..." This reflects too directly on the story's theme. The rest of the story holds the dissenter's paradox in productive tension; this passage states it.

**Suggestion**: Cut. The pigeon-counting beat already carries this weight implicitly.

---

## Strengths

### S1: Reverse chronology is expertly executed in Scenes 1-6

Each scene backward adds genuine recontextualization. The structure is not chronological rearrangement -- it is genuine mystery. The reader asks "why?" at each stage and gets the answer in the next scene backward.

### S2: The resignation letter as structural spine

Placing the letter second provides the intellectual framework for everything that follows. The other scenes demonstrate what the letter describes. Optimal positioning.

### S3: Scene 3 (Priya/gallery) is the strongest scene

The gap between Priya's shaking hands and Yuki's assessment recommendation is the story's most powerful demonstration of its theme. Shows rather than tells the letter's abstract argument.

### S4: Scene 6 (Michael in training data) is devastating

The 11-second pause, the recognition of Michael's notation, the revelation that "the telescope was partly made of the astronomer it had blinded" -- the emotional climax, perfectly positioned.

### S5: Portrait structure is appropriate

This is a character study rather than a transformation arc. The reverse chronology provides the mystery structure that makes the portrait dynamic. Right structural choice for this character.

### S6: Thematic coherence is sustained

Every scene builds the dissenter's paradox: moral clarity that is powerless to change the system. Held in productive tension (with the exception of Scene 7's explicit reflection, noted above).
