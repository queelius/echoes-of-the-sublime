## LOW Issues

### L1: "The desert was indifferent" -- slight register slip

**Location**: Line 21
**Confidence**: medium

"The desert was indifferent" is slightly too literary/interpretive for Amara's precise observational register. Her voice throughout is concrete and physical; attributing emotional states to landscapes is a narrative convention she would be more likely to avoid.

**Suggestion**: "The desert continued" or "The desert was still" -- observational rather than interpretive.

## Strengths

### S1: Amara's voice is consistently precise, warm, and controlled

The narrative voice achieves the canonical pattern throughout all modes -- narration, dialogue, and formal writing.

Key examples:
- Physical self-awareness: "The brightness felt physical, a pressure against her face" (line 7)
- Perceptual precision: "She counted them without deciding to. Thirty-seven. The number meant nothing. The counting meant everything" (line 15)
- Institutional analysis as perception: "The gap was the machine. The distance between a person's suffering and an institution's use of that suffering, maintained by language careful enough to make the distance feel natural" (line 89)
- Structural emotion: "She had cried until the crying became structural -- integrated, load-bearing, part of who she was" (line 209)

### S2: Letter achieves formal-document mode without losing Amara's voice

Direct-agency naming pattern in document form: "We identify people... We expand that capacity... people we damaged in pursuit of knowledge we decided was worth their minds." Active voice, named agents, moral precision. Same person as the narrator, shifted to formal register.

### S3: Thomas's voice is perfectly executed

- Characteristic delay: "Thomas walked four more steps before responding. This was his way" (line 103)
- Arm/sleeve gesture: "Thomas's hand moved to his left forearm. Not to the scars -- to the sleeve covering them" (line 113)
- Flat declarative: "I'm not arguing. I'm reporting." (line 127)
- Experience-verified facts: "By day nine the patterns were accumulating faster than I could drain them. By day fifteen I was bleeding through my nose" (line 119)
- Distinguished from Amara by syntactic flatness and absence of warmth

### S4: Yuki's voice is brief but precisely on-target

"Your ceiling is higher than we projected. The spike was clean. No cascade precursors. Your visual cortex recruitment is progressing faster than the standard timeline." Assessment-focused, data-oriented, no acknowledgment of Priya's distress. Distinguished from both Amara (who notices suffering) and Thomas (who reports it flatly).

### S5: Dialogue distinctiveness is excellent

All three speaking characters are immediately recognizable without tags:
- Amara: complete sentences, warmth, moral precision
- Thomas: flat, delayed, declarative, experience-based
- Yuki: imperative, assessment-focused, data-oriented

### S6: Metaphor families are consistent

Amara's metaphors are physical, structural, and perceptual:
- "sky so blue it looked algorithmic" (computational lens on nature)
- "crying became structural -- integrated, load-bearing" (emotion as architecture)
- "the surface feel like a membrane stretched over an abyss" (depth perception)
- "a bone heals stronger at the break" (physical process for emotional recovery)

### S7: Emotional register achieves "warm but controlled"

The child-drawing scene demonstrates warmth without sentimentality: "She felt a specific, located warmth." The precision of "specific, located" is Amara's; the warmth itself is genuine.
