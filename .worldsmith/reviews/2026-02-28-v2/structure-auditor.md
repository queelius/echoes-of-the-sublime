## Findings

### ch05 dissolution gradient -- structural position

The four-beat dissolution gradient across ch05 is structurally well-placed within Lena's arc:

1. **Beat 1 (line 97)**: Week 2, day 2. After the raven scene. Clinical intrusion about Morrison/raven comparison. Lena catches and corrects. She is still enough herself to recognize the clinical lens as wrong.

2. **Beat 2 (line 159)**: That night, alone. "Dissociative symptoms consistent with--" She catches and corrects, but the correction is weaker ("no. Not that. She was lonely") -- she reaches for the human word but it doesn't fit right. The self-correction is starting to feel performative.

3. **Beat 3 (line 173) -- NEW**: Same night, reading Ethan's text. "Attachment behavior, anxious subtype, escalating in frequency." No correction. She moves straight to "But she couldn't feel what he felt anymore." The clinical lens has become her default; she no longer notices it as abnormal.

4. **Beat 4 (line 265)**: Later that night, between waking and sleeping. Father's death flash, then "Residual affect from unprocessed material--manageable, non-recurring." No correction. She turns back to the sketches. The clinical lens now processes even grief over her dead father without resistance.

**Structural assessment**: The gradient is tightly compressed within a single chapter (ch05), which works because this chapter is the dissolution's tipping point -- Lena's mother has just had a stroke and Lena felt nothing. The four beats track the dissolution accelerating within hours, not weeks. Beat 3 is correctly placed between the self-aware correction (reading a text from a friend -- lower stakes than the raven/Morrison comparison) and the fully unconscious clinical processing (processing her father's death). The escalation of emotional intimacy (animals -> self-diagnosis -> friend -> father) mirrors the escalation of dissolution.

- **Severity**: No structural issues. The gradient is well-calibrated.

### ch04 Thomas beats -- pacing impact

The three Thomas beats (lines 189, 207, 259) are distributed across the training sequence in ch04:

- Beat 1 (189): During protein folding discussion
- Beat 2 (207): During "patterns become traps" discussion
- Beat 3 (259): During neural network perception discussion

These are spaced approximately 18-70 lines apart within a section that runs from line 135 (training room introduction) to line 270 (end of neural network section). They do NOT cluster. Each arrives at a natural pause in the technical content, providing a physical counterpoint to the intellectual exposition. This improves ch04's pacing, which was previously noted as heavy on exposition.

- **Severity**: No issues. Pacing improved.

### Hedging conversions -- narrative rhythm impact

Converting "began to" to direct verbs across 12 instances tightens the prose rhythm throughout Part I and early Part II. These chapters (1-5, 12) are Lena's most present-tense-feeling sections, where immediacy serves the narrative. The direct verbs sharpen action beats without affecting reflective passages (which were correctly left with their original construction).

- **Severity**: No issues. Rhythm improved.

### Anti-cliche compliance

- "No base case" count: 17 (style guide says ~18, acceptable)
- No new repetitions introduced by any of the changes
- The fragment technique ("The training session." / "The last three months.") is used only twice in ch11, which is not enough to register as a pattern -- good restraint

## Strengths Identified

1. The ch05 dissolution gradient is one of the manuscript's strongest structural devices. The compression of all four beats into a single chapter (rather than spreading across multiple chapters) creates an accelerating rhythm that mirrors the dissolution itself. The reader experiences the pace of Lena's loss.

2. The Thomas physical beats in ch04 break up the exposition rhythm without adding word count. They insert character presence into what would otherwise be a lecture sequence.

## Summary

All structural changes verified. No pacing issues introduced. The ch05 dissolution gradient is structurally sound and well-positioned within Lena's arc. The Thomas beats improve ch04's exposition-heavy pacing. Hedging conversions tighten narrative rhythm in Parts I-II. No anti-cliche violations.
