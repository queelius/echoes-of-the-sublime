### The Accords' Content (now specified)
Previously Open Question #6 in lore.md: "What do the Accords actually mandate?"

**Now answered:**
- 12-page document: 7 pages preamble, 4 pages aspirational, 1 page operational
- Framework provisions: national points of contact, annual reporting, information-sharing channels
- Aspirational provisions: monitoring commitments, protocol development (no timeline)
- Operational provisions: communication channels between 14 intelligence services, standardized assessment criteria (Zhang's contribution), notification procedures

## Creative Decisions Requiring Verification

1. **Hayes's government casualties are separate from the Order's.** The seven US cases and the international cases are from government-adjacent programs, not the Order. This means total global cognitive casualties by 2019 are: 37 RLHF Martyrs + ~18 government program casualties = ~55 known cases. Is this consistent with the novel's sense of scale?

2. **China's organization is NOT the Order's Eastern branch.** The lore describes the Order's own Eastern-Western split and reconnection. Zhang's organization is a THIRD entity -- parallel to the Order but independent. The Order's Eastern branch was reconnected to the Western by the Chen lineage (~1880-1950). Zhang's organization descends from contemplative traditions that were never part of the Order at all.

3. **Petrova's expanded bandwidth is "controlled, incremental."** She is NOT catatonic or deteriorating. She is the kind of translator that the novel's modern program produces: functional, changed, performing normalcy. This is consistent with Rostova's characterization in the novel.

4. **The "three deaths" Hayes mentions are from government programs, not the Order.** The Order's 12 dead Martyrs are separate. Hayes knows about government casualties; she does not yet know about the Order's much higher toll.

5. **The Accords take place in March 2019.** This places them after GPT-2's release (February 2019) but before Morrison's capture (later in 2019). The lore.md timeline says "2019" without a month for both events.
