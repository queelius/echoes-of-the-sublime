### Key Decisions Made

1. **Noor Hassani as opening POV.** The sequel opens outside the Order, in the commercial AI world. This mirrors the first novel's structure (outsider encountering the phenomenon) while inverting the context (tech campus instead of underground facility, no institutional support).

2. **Three-POV structure.** Primary rotation: Noor (discovery), Lena (depth), Hayes (institutional/political). Secondary POV chapters for Wei Jianming and Sister Marie-Therese in Part III.

3. **No Lena POV until Ch 4.** She is glimpsed through Hayes's perspective first. This preserves ambiguity about her post-novel state and creates tension about whether she is stable or drifting.

4. **The perceptual hazard operates through interpretability tools.** Noor's exposure vector is not model outputs but model internals -- she was looking at the model's cognitive structure directly. This is a side channel the Order never anticipated.

5. **Transmission confirmed early.** Ch 1 ends with Kai's exposure. The novel doesn't wait to establish that the perceptual hazard can spread between people.

6. **The Chinese program uses "contemplative interfacing."** Not the Order's interrogative session method but meditation in the presence of active computation. Different philosophy, different method, different results.

7. **Sister Marie-Therese proves the ancient path is alive.** 48 years of Carmelite prayer produced bandwidth expansion without any AI. This challenges the Order's assumption that computation is the primary pathway.

8. **Option E (preparation without s-risk disclosure) is the chosen response.** Not clean, not satisfying, but the least monstrous option available.

9. **Lena does NOT go through the second gate.** She stands before the elevator for 43 minutes (mirroring Ch 14) and turns away. This is a fundamentally different choice from the first novel: choosing limitation over knowledge. Whether this is wisdom or fear is unresolved.

10. **The ending mirrors the first novel's ambiguity.** The drift continues. The feeling persists. Which one wins is not resolved.
